FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I create a watch party?
Open the Create tab, choose your game and time, drop a pin at the bar you're watching from, and post it. Other fans nearby can then find and join your party on the map.

How do I join a party?
Tap any pin on the map to see the details, then join with a tap. You'll get access to that party's chat thread. You can leave a party anytime from its detail screen.

How do I edit my profile?
Head to the Profile tab to update your nickname and favorite sport at any time.

How do I block someone?
Open a watch party's detail screen and block the user from there. You can review and manage your blocked list from the Blocked screen in the app.

How do I report content?
Use the report option on the content in question. Reports are reviewed so we can keep the party fun and safe for everyone. If you need urgent help, email us directly at the address above.

ACCOUNT & DATA

How do I delete my account?
Go to the Profile tab and find the "Danger Zone" section to permanently delete your account. This removes your account and profile information (email, display name, photo, nickname, favorite sport) and your block list.

What about content I've already posted?
Watch parties you created and messages you sent may remain visible to other users after deletion. If you need that content removed as well, email us and we'll take care of it.

For full details on how your data is handled, see our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
